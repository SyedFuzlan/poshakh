<objective>
Add a `checkoutLimiter` rate limiter (20 req / 15 min window per IP) to the `/api/checkouts` route in server.js. The route currently has no rate limiter, allowing unlimited checkout saves — enabling phone number harvesting via cart abandon tracking or cart-spam attacks.

Purpose: `/api/checkouts` accepts unauthenticated writes (customers save their cart/contact info without logging in). Without a rate limiter, an attacker can POST thousands of requests to enumerate phone numbers or flood the orders pipeline. 20 per 15 minutes is appropriate for legitimate cart update behavior.

Output: server.js with checkoutLimiter defined alongside existing apiLimiter/authLimiter, applied to the /api/checkouts mount.
</objective>
